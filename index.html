Rural-urban disparities in clinical trial availability in the US, 2000–2025

Across 3,144 counties, rural counties had about one-third the per-capita availability of completed trials of urban counties (adjusted rate ratio 0.31, 95% CI 0.26–0.38). The gap narrowed from 2000 to 2025 but never closed.

A reusable tobacco phenotype for the OMOP Common Data Model

A point-in-time smoking-status algorithm built from standard concept sets, validated against survey self-report in 120,624 All of Us participants: moderate agreement for current smoking (kappa 0.45), good agreement for smoking duration (ICC 0.78).

Do rural patients drop out at each step from referral to transplant, and does it affect survival? A competing-risk study design in Epic Cosmos covering kidney, liver and pancreas referrals, 2014–2025.
