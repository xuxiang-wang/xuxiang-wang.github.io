Rural-Urban Disparities in Clinical Trial Availability in the US, 2000–2025

Rural counties had about one-third the per-capita availability of urban counties (adjusted RR, 0.31; 95% CI, 0.26-0.38), with the largest gap in the South (RR, 0.19; 95% CI, 0.14-0.25) and the smallest in the Northeast (RR, 0.54; 95% CI, 0.26-1.11). In this national analysis, rural US counties had persistently lower per-capita trial availability, consistent with research-infrastructure geography rather than social vulnerability.

A Computable OMOP Tobacco-Use Phenotype: Development and Validation Against All of Us Survey Data

This study presents an openly specified, standards-based, reproducible tobacco phenotype within the OMOP CDM, showing moderate agreement with self-reported survey data. For current smokers, kappa was 0.451 (95% CI 0.446-0.457; sensitivity 0.605, specificity 0.882, PPV 0.506). Agreement was good for smoking duration (ICC = 0.78) and poor for cigarettes per day (ICC = 0.19).
